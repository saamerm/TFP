Welcome to the Michigan Inventors Coalition

The Michigan Inventors Coalition (MIC) is a statewide 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to supporting Michigan's early-stage inventors and innovators as they move ideas toward commercialization. Founded in 2011, MIC connects inventors to critical resources through education, mentorship, and a collaborative network of local, statewide, and federal partners.

In partnership with Michigan State University and the EDA University Center, MIC hosts its annual Inventors Expo and year-round opportunities that bring together innovators, service providers, and industry experts—strengthening Michigan's innovation ecosystem and helping inventors access the guidance, connections, and opportunities they need to succeed.

About Us

The Michigan Inventors Coalition was founded in 2011 with a mission to foster innovation and support inventors across the state of Michigan. We believe that every great invention starts with an idea and the right support system to bring it to life.

Our Mission

To empower Michigan's inventors and innovators by providing resources, mentorship, and connections needed to transform ideas into successful commercial ventures.

Our Vision

A Michigan where every inventor has the support and resources needed to bring their innovations to market, driving economic growth and improving lives.

Our Impact

Since our founding, we've helped thousands of inventors connect with mentors, secure funding, and launch successful products that benefit communities across Michigan and beyond.

Want to Get Involved?

Join our community of innovators, mentors, sponsors, and volunteers who are shaping Michigan's future. Whether you're an inventor looking for support or someone who wants to give back to the innovation ecosystem, there's a place for you at MIC.

We're always looking for passionate individuals to volunteer at our events, mentor inventors, or join our committees. Your time and expertise can make a real difference in the lives of Michigan's innovators.

Pitch Competition

Watch the Competition

Watch the event and cheer on your favorite inventor. Join us for a competitive pitch event as inventors vie for cash prizes. Be a part of the action with real-time live audience voting. Attendance for the pitch competition is included with your attendee ticket.

Apply to Pitch

Are you an inventor who is eager to showcase your product? Apply to pitch and get a chance to win!

Application Criteria:

  • Must have a minimum viable product
  • Must be available to pitch in person at the 2026 Inventors Summit
  • Must be a Michigan resident or have a Michigan-based invention
  • Product must be at a stage where feedback and exposure would be beneficial
Apply to Pitch

Applications chaired by Meghan Hubbs and hosted by Velocity

Competition Details

Selected inventors will have 5 minutes to pitch their invention to a panel of judges and the live audience. Winners will be selected based on innovation, market potential, presentation quality, and audience votes.

Cash prizes will be awarded to the top three inventors, with additional recognition for honorable mentions.
