Welcome to the Michigan Inventors Coalition

The Michigan Inventors Coalition (MIC) is a statewide 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to supporting Michigan's early-stage inventors and innovators as they move ideas toward commercialization. Founded in 2011, MIC connects inventors to critical resources through education, mentorship, and a collaborative network of local, statewide, and federal partners.

In partnership with Michigan State University and the EDA University Center, MIC hosts its annual Inventors Expo and year-round opportunities that bring together innovators, service providers, and industry experts—strengthening Michigan's innovation ecosystem and helping inventors access the guidance, connections, and opportunities they need to succeed.

About Us

For over a decade, the Michigan Inventors Coalition has been at the forefront of Michigan's innovation landscape. Our mission is to foster a vibrant community where inventors can thrive, transform their ideas into viable products, and contribute to Michigan's economic growth.

We believe that great ideas can come from anyone, anywhere in Michigan. Our programs are designed to provide the support system necessary to navigate the complex journey from concept to market, including intellectual property guidance, prototyping assistance, business planning, and funding opportunities.

Through our annual Inventors Summit, regional workshops, one-on-one mentorship, and online resources, we've helped hundreds of Michigan inventors move closer to commercialization success.

Our Impact

Since our founding in 2011, MIC has:

  • Supported over 500 early-stage inventors and innovators
  • Connected inventors with more than 200 mentors and industry experts
  • Facilitated over $2M in funding for Michigan-based inventions
  • Hosted 13 successful annual Inventors Summits across Michigan
  • Built partnerships with 50+ organizations supporting innovation

Want to Get Involved?

MIC relies on the support of volunteers, sponsors, and community partners to deliver our programs and events. Whether you're an experienced inventor, a business professional, or simply passionate about innovation, there are many ways to contribute to Michigan's innovation ecosystem.

Volunteer opportunities include event planning, mentor matching, workshop facilitation, and day-of-event support for our annual Inventors Summit.
