Common Questions

How do you know which item is which?

Our app matches your uploaded photo and QR code to the physical item during the scan-and-match process at pickup.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We only use your QR code and address for the specific duration of the return transaction.

What if the return window is closed?

If the vendor won't take it, we can divert the item to one of our partner donation centers so it stays out of the landfill.
