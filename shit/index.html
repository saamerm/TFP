Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your account associated with S.H. I.T. summit created by The First Prototype, please follow the steps below:

Steps to Request Account Deletion:

  1. Send an email to saamer@thefirstprototype.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. In the email, include your account username and the email address associated with your account.
  3. State your request clearly to delete your account, and email us from the account you would like to delete.

Data Deletion and Retention Information:

Upon receiving your request, we will delete the following types of data:

  • Personal information (e.g., name, email address, username).
  • Usage data associated with your account.

We will retain the following data for legal and operational purposes:

  • Any data required to comply with legal obligations.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us at saamer@thefirstprototype.com.
